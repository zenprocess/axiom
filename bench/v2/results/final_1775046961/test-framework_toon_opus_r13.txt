Here's what the framework includes:

- **Assertions**: `assert_equal`, `assert_true`, `assert_false`, `assert_raises`, `assert_in`
- **Fixtures**: `@fixture` decorator registers callables by name; the runner auto-injects them by matching parameter names
- **Discovery**: `discover_tests()` finds all `test_*` callables on a module, class, or instance
- **Runner**: `run_tests()` executes discovered tests, prints PASS/FAIL/SKIP/ERR per test, and prints a summary line
- **Skip**: `@skip(reason="...")` decorator to skip tests
- **Demo**: the `__main__` block includes 7 sample tests (including a deliberate failure and a skip) to show it working
